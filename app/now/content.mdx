## Hisab

**Sawt is finished.** Sawt is Hisab's voice companion. People can talk to it in their own Arabic dialect, it answers in the same dialect, and it can read a bank statement back to them out loud.

**The first Hisab Circle cohort starts in November.** It is a free 12-week peer group for 20 people in the UAE who are paying off debt. They meet in small groups and use nicknames, so nobody has to share their real finances.
